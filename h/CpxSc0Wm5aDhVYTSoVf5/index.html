Before the party

  1. Wed Nov 4 (night): RSVPs close. Lock head count and assign the 20 main roles (drop Marshal, Taffy, Anna Belle, Billy or Bob if short); general roles beyond 20.
  2. ~Fri Nov 6: Character envelopes go out: invitation, Rules, Gazette (Oct 20, 1874) and the character description.
  3. Party week: print money on green paper, name tags, A/B sheets, exhibits, accusation sheets, awards; stuff and paperclip each bundle.

Night flow

  1. Stage 1 · 6:30 PM — Guests arrive
    Name tag, $500 mystery money and A envelope. Optional mug shot at the line-up board.
  2. Stage 2 — Introduction
    Harry High-Stakes reads the Introduction aloud: tournament over, Mitch Maverick won the $100,000 purse; bribery and blackmail encouraged.
  3. Mingling · 30–60 min
    Guests work their A objectives. Mitch quietly tells the host when he's done.
  4. Stage 3 — The murder
    While Harry awards the prize: lights out, loud bang (cap gun). Lights on: Mitch is down.
  5. Investigation
    Marshal outlines the body, reads the Investigation directions. Hand out all B envelopes (Exhibits B–G). No Marshal? Host outlines; the victim becomes Marshal Dalton.
  6. Guests investigate
    Marshal collects Exhibits A–G from Jesse, Holly, Billy/Marshal, Sally, Sam and Barb.
  7. Stage 4 — Evidence
    Marshal reads the Evidence script, holding up each exhibit. Hand out and collect accusation sheets.
  8. Stage 5 — Solution & awards
    Tally sheets, Marshal reads the solution. Awards: Smoking Gun, Dressed To Kill, Drama Queen, Richie Rich.

Props checklist

Host tips

  • Only two places reveal the killer: the Solution file and that character's B sheet.
  • Keep Introduction, Investigation, Evidence and Solution envelopes in order in a box.
  • Cast big talkers as Harry, Marshal Dalton, Mitch and Montgomery; give quieter kids exhibit-holding roles (Sally, Sam, Barb, Holly, Jesse).
  • Extra guests, in order: Cantina Girl A, Gambler A, Cantina Girl B, Gambler B, Traveler A, Cantina Girl C, Patron A, Cantina Girl D, Patron B, Traveler B, Patron C, Cantina Girl E, Patron D, Traveler C.
⚠ Host only – spoilers (click to reveal the solution)

Murderer: Montgomery Money.

Telegram: sent to Jesse Thursday, Oct 19 at 8:49 AM from St. Louis, when everyone but Montgomery was already in Deadwood.

Gun: prints show a left-handed shooter. Jesse's left hand is bandaged, so Jesse couldn't fire it. Montgomery wears his watch on his right wrist: he's left-handed.

Motive: the clipping shows Montgomery was conned by “Victer Hammick” — an anagram of Mitch Maverick. Vigilante payback, still unlawful.

Montgomery's B sheet: told he's the killer; keep it secret, blame Harry and the bank robber, drink with his left hand.
